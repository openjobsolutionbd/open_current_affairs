# Short Notes

## Global Value Chain

Global Value Chain (GVC) refers to an international production system where different stages of producing a product—such as design, production, assembly, marketing and distribution—are carried out in different countries, with each stage adding value to the final product. Instead of producing goods entirely in one country, firms divide production tasks across countries based on cost, skills and technology. The concept of GVC began developing in the early 1990s and was first systematically introduced by Gary Gereffi in 1994 through his work on global commodity chains. By the mid-1990s to early 2000s, the term GVC became widely used in research and policy discussions. Its growth has been supported by trade liberalization, lower transport costs, improved logistics and advances in information and communication technology (ICT). GVCs help countries, especially developing nations, gain foreign investment, technology, employment and export opportunities. For example, an iPhone may be designed in the United States, components produced in Japan and South Korea and assembled in China. However, GVCs also involve risks, such as dependence on foreign firms and remaining in low-value activities for sustainable development.

## Brain Circulation

Brain circulation is the continuous movement of skilled professionals across countries, enabling them to acquire education, experience and global networks abroad while contributing to their home country through research, innovation, investment, entrepreneurship, collaboration, or return migration. Unlike brain drain—the permanent loss of skilled workers due to better career prospects—brain circulation turns global mobility into a driver of national development. Its major benefits include technology and knowledge transfer, remittances and foreign direct investment (FDI), human capital development (brain gain), and stronger trade and business networks. Countries such as China, South Korea, Japan, Ireland, and Taiwan have successfully transformed overseas talent into national assets by investing in strong universities, research ecosystems, and merit-based institutions. The government aims to turn 'brain drain' into 'brain circulation' by involving non-resident Bangladeshi experts in national development in FY 2026-27. The country should redesign overseas PhD scholarships to include joint supervision, Bangladeshi institutional affiliation, collaborative research, publications, and regular engagement with local universities. Such reforms would strengthen national research capacity while scholars are abroad and help convert brain drain into brain circulation, fostering long-term innovation, productivity and sustainable economic growth.

## Bangla QR

Bangla QR is an interoperable QR-based digital payment system introduced by Bangladesh Bank under the National Payment Switch Bangladesh (NPSB). Launched in 18 January 2023, it enables customers of participating banks, Mobile Financial Services (MFS), Payment Service Providers (PSPs), and Payment System Operators (PSOs) to make payments using a single, unified QR code. Unlike the previous system, where each bank or MFS used its own QR code, Bangla QR allows customers of bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or any participating bank to pay through the same QR code, improving interoperability and convenience. To accelerate digital payments, Bangladesh Bank made Bangla QR mandatory at all merchant points from 1 July 2026, replacing proprietary QR codes. Bangla QR offers a secure, low-cost, and contactless payment solution. It eliminates the need for expensive Point-of-Sale (POS) machines, as merchants can accept payments with a simple QR sticker. The initiative aims to reduce cash transactions, expand digital payments, enhance transparency, strengthen financial inclusion, and build a cashless, technology-driven economy. Although implementation faces challenges such as technical integration, transaction settlement, revenue-sharing, and QR replacement costs, Bangla QR is expected to become the foundation of Bangladesh's unified digital payment ecosystem.
